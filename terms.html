TERMS & COMMERCIAL CLARITY

Planning estimates are useful—not binding quotations.

We want customers to understand what is included, what is estimated, and what must be confirmed before a paid engagement begins.

1. AI Solution Engine output

The public AI Solution Engine provides an initial business-value, learning, implementation, budget and scheduling plan based on the information supplied by the visitor. It is a planning aid and not professional, legal, financial, security or regulatory advice.

2. Estimates and budgets

Any budget range shown by the website is an indicative planning estimate in Canadian dollars unless stated otherwise. Final pricing depends on confirmed scope, number of participants, delivery format, customization, technology environment, travel, software/licensing requirements, data sensitivity, integration complexity and support requirements.

3. Standard training pricing

Published standard-training fees are before applicable taxes. Virtual delivery is included unless stated otherwise. On-site travel, venue costs, software licences, specialized labs, custom case development and major content customization are quoted separately. Dates and availability must be confirmed before booking.

4. Responsible use

Do not enter confidential, regulated, personal, client, proprietary or security-sensitive information into the public planning interface. A secure process should be agreed before sharing sensitive business information.

5. Human review

Recommendations involving governance, security, regulated data, architecture, procurement, contractual commitments or material investment should be reviewed with the appropriate organizational stakeholders before implementation.

6. Brand and communications

Enterprise AI Skills is the specialist client-facing AI business-value, workforce capability and training brand. Selected AliTechGrid Canada business communication channels are used for client service and operations.

7. Confirmation

A paid engagement begins only after scope, deliverables, schedule and commercial terms have been confirmed with the client.

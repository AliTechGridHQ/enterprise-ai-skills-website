# Deploy to Cloudflare Pages

## Fastest method: direct upload

1. Sign in to Cloudflare.
2. Open **Workers & Pages**.
3. Select **Create application** → **Pages** → **Upload assets**.
4. Upload the contents of this website folder or the ZIP file.
5. Name the project `enterprise-ai-skills`.
6. After deployment, open **Custom domains**.
7. Add `enterpriseaiskills.com`.
8. Add `www.enterpriseaiskills.com` and redirect it to the root domain.
9. Add `enterpriseaitraining.ca` later and redirect it to the primary site or a Canadian landing page.

## Launch checks

- HTTPS is active.
- The main domain loads without `www`.
- Mobile navigation works.
- Every email link opens the correct address.
- Legal corporation name is displayed.
- Privacy and terms pages are reviewed.
- No unsupported certification, partnership or accreditation claim appears.
